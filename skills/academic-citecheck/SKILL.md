# Academic citecheck

Call AutoCite in Whitepages mode and retain footnote/endnote locations. Keep `supra`, `supra note`, and `hereinafter` ambiguity visible. Identify the exact retrieved local chunk IDs behind an explanation. Do not invent archive URLs, authors, titles, dates, pincites, or parentheticals. Apply only mechanical `safe_auto_fix` output and request approval for nonmechanical changes.

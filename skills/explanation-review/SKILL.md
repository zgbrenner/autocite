# Explanation and correction review

Use `explain_issue` and `get_rule_context`; do not reconstruct rules from memory. Cite exact local chunk IDs and distinguish deterministic logic, parsed structure, retrieval, model inference, external verification, and ambiguity. Never describe a retrieval score as a legal conclusion. Ask approval before applying suggested or nonmechanical changes and preserve non-citation prose.

# Citation signals

Signal spelling, punctuation, grouping, and attachment can be reviewed mechanically. Whether an authority actually supports, contradicts, or analogizes to the proposition requires source analysis and legal judgment.

# Explanatory parentheticals

AutoCite can inspect location, capitalization, punctuation, and balanced nesting. It must not claim that a parenthetical accurately characterizes a source without source review.

# Pincites and quotations

Direct quotations and source-specific propositions ordinarily warrant a precise location. Missing page facts must produce a review warning, not an invented pincite.

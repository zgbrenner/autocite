## Reuse rules

- One core idea can be adapted to LinkedIn, X, email, and a short demo, but the opening and action should fit the channel.
- Do not post identical copy across every platform at the same time.
- Use real product screens, citation examples, evaluation artifacts, or diagrams. Avoid generic gavels, courthouse stock photos, and AI-brain imagery.
- Never expose confidential documents or user-provided examples without written permission.
- Turn substantive criticism into documentation, tests, or public issues and reference the improvement later.

## Sustainable cadence after day 30

- One substantive guide or changelog article every two weeks.
- One product or methodology demonstration each week.
- One community question or adversarial test request each week.
- One release recap whenever meaningful functionality ships.
- No daily posting requirement when there is nothing useful to add.

# AutoCite Positioning

## Category

**Private legal citation checker**

AutoCite should lead with the category ordinary users understand, not the implementation surface. MCP, deterministic rules, citation graphs, and local SLMs support the promise but should not replace it in the first sentence.

## Core promise

> Fix supported Bluebook citation problems without rewriting your document or uploading confidential legal work.

## One-sentence pitch

AutoCite is an open-source, local-first legal citation checker that preserves legal prose, applies only supported mechanical fixes automatically, and shows what still requires human judgment.

## Audience-specific value

### Law students

Catch mechanical citation mistakes before a memo, seminar paper, note, or write-on deadline without turning the assignment into chatbot-generated prose.

### Law-review editors

Review long drafts, footnotes, and short forms with document-wide relationships, exact issue decisions, and transparent unresolved questions.

### Lawyers and clerks

Review filing citations locally with practitioner-focused checks, source preservation, and an audit trail rather than a silent rewrite.

### Legal technologists

Adopt an inspectable, deterministic-first citation layer through desktop, MCP, CLI, or authenticated local APIs.

## Message hierarchy

1. Private legal citation checker.
2. Leaves non-citation prose alone.
3. Standard desktop review stays local and has no application telemetry.
4. Deterministic validation controls automatic edits.
5. Bluepages and Whitepages workflows.
6. Exact spans, decisions, provenance, and exports.
7. Open source and auditable.
8. Explicit limitations.

## Proof points

- MIT-licensed public source.
- DOCX, searchable PDF, Markdown, and TXT workflows.
- Local desktop review with no application telemetry.
- Exact-span validation before edits.
- Conservative document-wide short-form relationships.
- Published evaluations and rule coverage.
- Release checksums and manifests when attached to an artifact.
- Optional CourtListener retrieval only when explicitly enabled.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim or imply:

- complete or guaranteed Bluebook compliance;
- guaranteed accuracy;
- good-law checking or citator equivalence;
- proposition-support conclusions;
- controlling-authority analysis;
- court, school, journal, law-firm, Bluebook, CourtListener, or Free Law Project endorsement;
- that development work has shipped before a release is published;
- that every feature is offline when optional network features exist.

## Preferred phrases

- private legal citation checker
- local-first legal citation reviewer
- supported mechanical citation fixes
- preserves non-citation prose
- exact citation spans
- review-required issue
- optional source retrieval
- published limitations

## Avoid

- AI lawyer
- perfect citations
- one-click Bluebook compliance
- never make a citation mistake again
- replaces cite checking
- validates your legal argument
- guarantees good law

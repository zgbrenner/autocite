# AutoCite Media and Asset System

## Visual direction

- True white and deep ink.
- Restrained blue accent.
- Serif editorial headings and sans-serif interface text.
- Real document and issue-review UI.
- Thin rules, paper structure, exact spans, and evidence drawers.
- No courthouse stock imagery, gavels, scales of justice, robot heads, neon AI gradients, or fake customer logos.

1. Homepage desktop screenshot at 1440 × 900.

5. Methodology diagram showing local review and optional CourtListener retrieval.
6. Open Graph image at 1200 × 630.

8. Square social avatar and favicon.
9. Transparent AutoCite wordmark and brand-mark SVG.
10. Short silent demo clip with captions.

## Screenshot rules

- Use fictional authorities or clearly public examples.
- Never show privileged, client, employer, journal-submission, or unpublished third-party text.
- Keep browser chrome clean and remove personal bookmarks, tabs, account names, and notifications.
- Do not fabricate product states that the current public release cannot reproduce.
- Label development-interface screenshots as previews until released.
- Include the limitations panel in at least one product screenshot.

## Demo sample

Use this fictional sample unless a better public-domain example is approved:

```text
The complaint alleges a claim under 42 USC §1983. Smith controls. id at 172.
```

The browser preview may demonstrate:

```text
42 USC §1983 → 42 U.S.C. § 1983
id at 172 → Id. at 172
```

State clearly that the browser preview is deliberately limited and does not verify the authority or proposition.

## Accessibility

- Provide descriptive alt text for every informative image.
- Do not place essential copy only inside an image.
- Caption videos and describe the result of each action.
- Maintain WCAG-compliant contrast.
- Avoid rapid cursor motion and respect reduced-motion preferences.

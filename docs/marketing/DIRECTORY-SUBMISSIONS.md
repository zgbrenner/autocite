# AutoCite Directory and Community Submission Checklist

Submit only after the production URL, current release, screenshots, and claims are verified.

## Developer and open-source channels

- GitHub repository topics and social preview.
- Hacker News Show HN.
- Product Hunt.
- AlternativeTo, when the listing accurately distinguishes local review from cloud products.
- Open-source software directories.
- MCP registries that accept legal productivity tools.
- Python and developer newsletters after a stable package release.

## Legal technology channels

- Legal-tech newsletters and independent reviewers.
- Law-library and legal-research technology groups.
- Legal-writing instructor communities.
- Law-review and journal editor communities.
- Bar-association technology committees where self-promotion is permitted.
- Solo and small-firm technology communities.

## Law-school distribution

- Legal-writing faculty review request.
- Law librarians and research instructors.
- Journal executive boards and cite-check coordinators.
- Student technology and entrepreneurship groups.
- Accessibility and academic-support offices for usability feedback.

## Submission packet

Provide:

1. product name and preferred descriptor;
2. one-sentence and short descriptions from `POSITIONING.md`;
3. production homepage and latest release;
4. source repository and license;
5. one desktop screenshot and one scanner screenshot;
6. privacy and methodology links;
7. explicit limitations and non-affiliation statement;
8. founder contact route;
9. date last verified.

## Qualification questions

Before submitting, confirm:

- The directory permits open-source or free products.
- The category is relevant to legal citation review.
- The listing does not require an inaccurate “AI accuracy” percentage.
- The directory is not selling fake rankings, backlinks, reviews, or votes.
- The terms do not claim broad rights in uploaded product assets.
- The contact details can be maintained over time.

## Do not use

- Paid backlink farms.
- Fake Product Hunt votes or GitHub stars.
- Automated Reddit account networks.
- Fabricated testimonials or customer logos.
- Directory descriptions that promise complete Bluebook compliance.
- Scholarship-link schemes unrelated to the product.
- Mass cold email to students or lawyers without relevance and opt-out controls.

## Tracking sheet fields

- Directory or community.
- Submission URL.
- Audience.
- Date submitted.
- Listing owner.
- Description version.
- Screenshot version.
- Status.
- Follow-up date.
- Referral traffic after privacy-safe analytics is approved.
- Notes or corrections required.

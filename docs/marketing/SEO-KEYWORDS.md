## Internal-link rules

- Every guide links to one relevant product page, methodology or privacy, the risk scan, and the download source when natural.
- Product pages link to methodology, privacy, comparison, guides, and download.
- Avoid exact-match anchor repetition across every page.
- Do not create location or jurisdiction doorway pages without verified, jurisdiction-specific substance.

## Content quality requirements

- Answer the query before introducing AutoCite.
- Distinguish mechanical citation review from legal conclusions.
- Include a useful checklist, decision rule, or workflow.
- Cite official or primary external sources when making time-sensitive legal or technical claims.
- Update publication dates only when content materially changes.
- Never inflate word count with repeated keyword variants.

## Measurement plan

When analytics are deliberately added, track privacy-safe aggregate events only:

- organic landing page;
- risk-scan completion count, without input text;
- outbound release-page clicks;
- guide-to-product click-through;
- returning visitor share, without fingerprinting.

Do not add analytics until the implementation, disclosure, retention, and opt-out choices are approved.
